Our Heritage

Milora Home Exclusive bridges the gap between traditional Turkish textile craftsmanship and modern global interior design. With our historical roots in Bursa, Turkey, and our global sales office in Chicago, USA, we are uniquely positioned to serve top-tier interior designers, furniture manufacturers, and hospitality projects globally.

Our signature collections are woven exclusively for us at our partner mill in Bursa, the historical heart of the Turkish textile industry. By holding our stock directly in Turkey, we maintain strict quality control and ensure that our intricate jacquards, rich velvets, and elegant plains are dispatched worldwide within 3-5 business days.
